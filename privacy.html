Privacy

How this site handles your information.

Short version: this site collects nothing on its own. The only information it receives is what you type into the pilot access form, and that is used only to answer you.

This site

scryware.tech is the public website for Scryware Command. It sets no tracking cookies and runs no analytics. Browsing it sends nothing to Scryware.

The pilot form

The pilot access form asks for your name, email, company or organization, team size (optional), and a short message about what you would pilot. When you submit it, the form is delivered to kevin@scryware.io through FormSubmit, a form-delivery service. The information is used only to read and answer your request. It is never sold, rented, or shared for marketing.

Email

Writing to kevin@scryware.io directly gets the same treatment: your message is used to answer you, and nothing more.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub keeps standard server logs, which can include IP addresses, as part of running the service.

Removing your request

To have a submitted pilot request deleted, email kevin@scryware.io from the address you used on the form and it will be removed.
